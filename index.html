How PAKX works

Every PAKX comes from Hive Power that someone delegated. That HP is never powered down. It keeps working, and part of what it earns comes back to holders as liquid HIVE.

  1. Delegate Hive Power to @pakx. Your HP stays yours, and you can undelegate whenever you like.
  2. Receive PAKX every day. New PAKX is minted from the curation rewards your HP earns, starting about 7 days after you delegate. After you undelegate, PAKX keeps arriving for 7 more days.
  3. @pakx puts all its HP to work. Curation rewards are always powered up and delegated to @hivepakistan, a manual curation project that supports many Hive communities.
  4. Returns split in two. Half is paid out as liquid HIVE dividends to PAKX holders. The other half is powered up and delegated again, so future dividends have more behind them.

What holders get

Daily liquid HIVE

Hold PAKX in your Hive-Engine wallet and dividends arrive daily as liquid HIVE. The amount depends on curation and trading results, so it changes over time.

Nominate other creators

Comment !PAKX on a post you think deserves support. Nominations bring a small upvote and put the post in front of @hivepakistan curators.

Holding more PAKX unlocks more nominations a day. Thresholds change as the holder base grows, so current tiers are posted on Discord.

Mint by delegating

No need to buy on the market. Delegating HP mints new PAKX straight to your wallet, and your HP stays in your account.

Built to get scarcer

Minting tightens over time. Hive’s inflation shrinks year by year, and PAKX follows the same logic: every PAKX is backed by more than 2 HP, and each new PAKX takes more delegated HP to create than the last.

Trading profits burn supply. The @pakx-burn account trades PAKX on the open market. Half of its profits are burned by sending PAKX to @null, and half grow the trading account. Fewer tokens means the same dividends are shared among fewer holders.

A buyback fund. 10% of every mint goes to @pakx-fund, which uses its dividends to buy PAKX from the market.

The engines behind PAKX

Beyond curation, PAKX is supported by trading systems that run around the clock. Exact strategies stay private so they can’t be copied or gamed, but every account trades in public, so results can be checked.

How nominations stay fair

PAKX isn’t a tipping token. !PAKX is a way for holders to spotlight other creators, so a few rules keep it honest.

Two kinds of nomination. Semi-automated nominations carry a smaller vote and can be revoked if abused. Manual-consideration nominations are reviewed by hand and backed by more HP.

No self-nominations. The point is to help others get discovered.

Nothing is forgotten. If a nominated post is skipped, the author’s nominations are still counted for future consideration.

The details stay private on purpose. Rules that aren’t public can’t be gamed, and they can change if anyone tries.
